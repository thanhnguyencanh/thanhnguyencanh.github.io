Thanh Nguyen Canh

PhD student in Information Science

Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Information Science at the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST). I work at the Robotics Laboratory, advised by Prof. Nak Young Chong, on 3D active semantic SLAM.

I received my master's degree in Information Science from JAIST, Japan, and my B.S. degree in Robotics Engineering from the University of Engineering and Technology, Vietnam National University. My master's work focused on real-time semantic-aware simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).

During my master's studies, I collaborated with Prof. Xiem HoangVan, Prof. Armagan Elibol, Prof. Manh Duong Phung, and Prof. Van-Truong Nguyen.
