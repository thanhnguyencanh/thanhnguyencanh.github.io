Topics across venues

The share of each venue's papers in its last two editions that name a topic. Select a column to open that venue's trends.

Data: titles, years and venues from the dblp computer science bibliography (CC0), downloaded 2026-11-10, the same data as the Paper Explorer; workshop papers are not included. A venue's latest year is the last one in dblp: 2026 for ICRA, RA-L, T-RO, IJRR, Sci. Robotics, CVPR, ECCV and ICLR; 2025 for IROS, ICCV, NeurIPS and ICML; 2024 for RSS and CoRL. Topics come from keyword rules on titles, so a share is the share of titles that name the topic. Terms are words and two-word phrases of titles, with plurals folded and function words removed; words that describe a paper's format, such as “Position:” or “Demonstrating”, are left out. Rising and fading terms and topic shifts compare a venue's last two editions with its last two editions at least four years earlier. Journal volumes of the current year are incomplete.
